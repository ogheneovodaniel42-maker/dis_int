Our Story

Daniel International School

The Story of Daniel International School Daniel International School has its foundation in the vision of its founder, Daniel S. Gidoti. His vision was to provide an education that develops the whole person—mind, body, character, and spirit—while recognizing the potential of every student. That philosophy continues to guide DIS-Gidoti today. At the age of twenty-two, Daniel S. Gidoti founded Daniel S. Gidoti Preparatory School for boys, welcoming its first twenty-five students. From its beginning, the school was built on a commitment to learning, discipline, strong character, and the development of the whole student. As the institution grew, Ogheneovo S. Preparatory School was established for girls, extending the educational opportunities available within the Gidoti school community. Together, the two schools developed their own traditions while remaining grounded in the same commitment to excellence, character, and purposeful education. The institution continued to develop its educational programs and facilities, including a growing focus on technology and innovation. These developments reflected a commitment to preparing students with the knowledge, skills, confidence, and values required for a changing world. In 2020, the schools were brought together to form Daniel International School. The unified institution became known by its short name, DIS-Gidoti, bringing the traditions and educational values of the schools together under one identity. Ogheneovo Daniel serves as Head of School, continuing to lead the institution with a focus on academic excellence, character development, discipline, innovation, and the long-term success of its students. Today, DIS-Gidoti remains committed to the founding vision of Daniel S. Gidoti: educating the whole person and preparing students to lead with knowledge, integrity, confidence, compassion, and purpose.

  • Ogheneovo S. Preparatory School

    Ogheneovo S. Preparatory School was established as the girls’ school within the Gidoti educational tradition. It was created to provide girls with the same commitment to academic excellence, personal development, character, and purposeful education that defined the boys’ school. The school became an important part of the institution’s history and contributed to the foundation of the unified Daniel International School.

  • Ogheneovo S. Preparatory School

    Ogheneovo S. Preparatory School continued the institution’s commitment to providing girls with a strong educational foundation. Through its development, the school became an important part of the Gidoti legacy and helped establish the foundation for the later union of the schools. Its history remains an important part of the story of today’s Daniel International School.

  • DIS-Gidoti

    The two schools eventually came together, bringing their shared traditions, values, and commitment to education into one institution. In 2020, the schools were united to form Daniel International School, known by its short name, DIS-Gidoti. The union created a unified school community while preserving the educational legacy established by Daniel S. Gidoti. Today, Ogheneovo Daniel serves as Head of School, building on the institution’s founding principles and leading DIS-Gidoti toward continued academic excellence, character development, innovation, and service. Daniel S. Gidoti’s founding philosophy was centered on educating the whole person—mind, body, character, and spirit. That principle remains an important part of the identity and educational purpose of DIS-Gidoti.

DIS GIDOTI LIBRARIES

Media Center Resources

Welcome to the DIS-Daniel Library Media Center. The Harris Library services K3-4th grade students; the Roberts Library services 5th-12th grade students. Students can visit our libraries to access digital resources, collaborate and work together, learn 21st century skills, and instill a love of reading.

Overdue or Lost Books

K3 through 1st grade students check out one book, and 2nd-4th grade students can check out two books at a time from the lower school library. There is not a fine for overdue books. However, your child will not be able to check out another book until the book is returned. If your child loses or damages a book, the fee is $15. Once they pay the lost/damaged fee they will be allowed to check out again.

AR Renaissance Home Connect

First, second, third, and fourth grade students use a software program called Renaissance at school. It is what they use to take AR tests. This program has a special feature called Home Connect, which you can access outside of school from any computer with an internet connection. Home Connect allows you to view information about student progress. It also allows you to sign up to receive email notifications when your child completes an activity or assessment at school. The login for Home Connect is the same login students use at school to take AR tests on their ipads. https://global-zone51.renaissance-go.com/studentprogress/homeconnect?t=79629

Welcome to the Roberts Library

The Roberts Library is located in the Upper School and serves students in both middle and upper school. Grades 5-12 visit independently, in groups, participate in club meetings and collaborative class projects.  Students have access to print resources located in the library and digital resources using the links on this page to support them in their research and learning.  June 1st students will be charged $20 per book not returned by the end of the school year or lost during the school year.

ACADEMICS

Inspiring Excellence Through Purposeful Learning

At Daniel International School, our academic programme is designed to develop the whole student—building knowledge, character, confidence, creativity, and the skills needed to succeed in a changing world. From the early years through secondary school, students are encouraged to think deeply, learn with purpose, and pursue excellence.

  • Academic Excellence

    A strong academic foundation that challenges students to think, understand, apply, and achieve.

  • Whole-Student Development

    Learning that supports intellectual, personal, social, physical, and character development.

  • Future Readiness

    Opportunities to develop creativity, critical thinking, digital awareness, communication, and leadership skills.

Preschool / Elementary School

  • Preschool / Pre-K

    Our early years programme provides a caring and engaging environment where children build confidence, curiosity, communication, early literacy, numeracy, social skills, and positive learning habits. Students are guided through age-appropriate activities that encourage discovery, creativity, independence, and strong character.

  • Learning & Enrichment

    Students experience a balanced programme that develops foundational skills while encouraging creativity, physical development, collaboration, and a love of learning. Classroom experiences are supported by purposeful enrichment that helps every child discover and develop their strengths.

  • Foundational Learning

    Teachers provide close guidance and meaningful learning experiences that help students strengthen reading, writing, mathematics, communication, problem-solving, and critical-thinking skills. The focus is on building a strong foundation for continued academic growth.

        Student Guidance & Wellbeing

        • The Student Guidance and Wellbeing programme supports students throughout their school journey. Guidance focuses on helping students develop healthy relationships, confidence, responsibility, positive decision-making, and effective learning habits. Students can receive age-appropriate support as they navigate academic, personal, social, and developmental challenges, while families and teachers are encouraged to work together in supporting each student.

        University & Career Guidance

        • Overview

          The University & Career Guidance programme helps students and families make informed plans for life after secondary school. Students are supported in exploring higher-education options, career interests, application requirements, personal goals, and the practical steps involved in preparing for their next stage.

        • 12th Grade

          Students receive focused guidance as they prepare for life after secondary school. Support may include planning for university or other post-secondary pathways, preparing application materials, identifying opportunities, and making informed decisions about their next step.

        • 11th Grade

          Students begin more detailed post-secondary planning, exploring areas of study, career interests, admission requirements, and the steps needed to prepare for applications and future opportunities.

        • 10th Grade

          Students are encouraged to explore academic interests, career possibilities, and different pathways for further education. Guidance helps them understand their strengths and begin setting meaningful goals.

        • 9th Grade

          As students begin High School, guidance introduces goal-setting, academic planning, personal development, and the importance of building a strong record of learning and participation.

        • 8th Grade

          Students are introduced to future-focused conversations that help them understand their interests, strengths, responsibilities, and the opportunities available as they progress into High School.

        • 7th Grade

          Students begin developing awareness of their strengths, interests, study habits, and future possibilities while learning how present choices can support long-term goals.

          Academic Support

          • Mission

            Academic Support is designed to help students access learning effectively and make steady academic progress. Support is responsive to individual learning needs and is provided in partnership with students, families, teachers, and school leadership.

          • Goal

            Our goal is to help every student build confidence, strengthen learning skills, overcome academic challenges, and reach their highest potential. Support is focused on practical strategies that help students become increasingly independent learners.

          • Individual Learning Plan

            Where additional support is needed, the school works with relevant members of the learning community to understand a student’s strengths and areas for growth. Appropriate strategies, classroom support, monitoring, and communication with families can then be used to provide a more personalised learning experience.

          • Get Started

            Without curriculum modification, the Academic Support Program provides supplemental assistance for students through two distinct models of instruction: Guided Study and Second Delivery. For more information on the models of instruction, please contact the Director of Academic Support, Courtney Peak.

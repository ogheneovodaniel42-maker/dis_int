Arts

Creativity gives every student the freedom to imagine, express, and create.

At DIS-Gidoti, the arts inspire confidence, creativity, discipline, and meaningful self-expression.

ART PROGRAMS

Creativity is an essential part of a well-rounded education. At DIS-Gidoti, students are encouraged to explore their talents, express their ideas, and discover the power of the arts.

  • Art & Photography

    Students are encouraged to explore drawing, painting, design, photography, and other visual forms of creative expression. Through practical projects and guided instruction, students develop artistic skills, confidence, observation, imagination, and an appreciation for creativity.

  • BAND

    Our instrumental music programme gives students opportunities to learn, practise, perform, and grow through music. Students develop musical technique, teamwork, discipline, listening skills, and confidence while discovering the joy of making music together.

  • Chorus & Vocal Music

    Choral music provides students with opportunities to develop their voices, musical understanding, confidence, and ability to perform as part of an ensemble. Students learn the importance of preparation, listening, harmony, cooperation, and expressive performance.

  • Drama & Theatre

    Drama and theatre give students a platform to develop confidence, communication, creativity, collaboration, and stage presence. Through rehearsals, performances, storytelling, and creative production, students learn to express ideas and work effectively as a team.

  • Creative Writing & Publications

    Students can develop their voices through creative writing, journalism, school publications, photography, and other forms of storytelling. These experiences strengthen communication, research, editing, creativity, and responsible expression.

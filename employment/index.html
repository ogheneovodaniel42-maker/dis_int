Employment Opportunities

At DIS-Gidoti Preparatory School, we believe that exceptional educators and staff are the foundation of a vibrant and thriving school community. Joining DIS-Gidoti means becoming part of a collaborative, mission-driven environment that is deeply committed to academic excellence, character development, and lifelong learning. By joining our team, you are not just joining a workplace; you are becoming part of a community that strives to inspire, support, and grow together.

Lower School

  • Extended Care Worker (K3–5th Grade)

    DIS-Gidoti Preparatory School seeks enthusiastic, dependable, and student-centered individuals to join our Extended Care Program. This position is ideal for candidates who enjoy working with children and are interested in gaining experience in education, childcare, or youth development.

    Extended Care Workers support students in a safe, nurturing, and engaging after-school environment while upholding DIS-Gidoti’s mission to educate students in mind, body, and spirit.

    This is a primarily Monday–Friday position, typically from 2:30 p.m. to 5:30 p.m., working with a specific age group ranging from K3 through 5th grade. Flexible scheduling is available for candidates currently enrolled in undergraduate programs.

Middle School

  • Middle School 8th Grade American History Maternity Substitute Teacher November -December 2026

    DIS-Gidoti Preparatory School seeks a dynamic, engaging, and student-centered 8th Grade American History Substitute Teacher during November and December of the 2026–2027 academic year. The ideal candidate will demonstrate a passion for history and inquiry-based learning, a commitment to academic excellence, and the ability to inspire students to think like historians through the critical analysis of primary and secondary sources. By implementing pre-designed lesson plans and pacing, this teacher will guide students in developing historical thinking skills by emphasizing evidence-based reasoning, contextual understanding, and the construction of well-supported arguments. Instruction will center on argumentative writing, collaborative discussions, and the thoughtful examination of diverse perspectives, preparing students to engage deeply with the past and make meaningful connections to the present.

OUR STUDENTS

At DIS-Gidoti, we want our students to become confident learners and responsible members of their community. We encourage them to develop in four important areas: the mind, the body, the spirit, and character.

  • MIND

    Curious and Independent

    DIS-Gidoti students are encouraged to ask questions, understand what they learn, and approach challenges with confidence. We help them develop good study habits, think independently, solve problems, and stay open to new ideas.

  • BODY

    Discipline and Teamwork

    Challenged physically and motivated to persevere with courage and determination. The body of a DIS-Gidoti student is disciplined and devoted with a focus on teamwork and the pursuit of collective goals.

  • SPIRIT

    Purpose and Responsibility

    Internally driven with confidence and spiritual wisdom that exudes humility and compassion. The spirit of a DIS-Gidoti student is authentic and empathetic.

  • CHARACTER

    Respect and Character

    Grounded purposefully with a genuine passion and commitment to the improvement of self, school, and community. The character of a DIS-Gidoti student is honorable and respectful with a heart for servant leadership.

Summer & Auxiliary Programs

Activities for Year-Round Development

DIS-Daniel Summer & Auxiliary Programs build on the mission of our school by providing academic, athletic, and creative opportunities to ignite new passions and strengthen an individual’s potential in mind, body, spirit, and character. Our 53-acre campus provides an expansive environment that lays the foundation for encouraging children to challenge themselves academically, creatively, and physically throughout the summer.

SUMMER PROGRAMS

  • DIS-Daniel Summer Programs are an extension of DIS-Daniel Preparatory School. We build on the mission of our school by providing academic, athletic, and creative opportunities to ignite new passions and strengthen an individual’s potential in mind, body, spirit, and character. Our 53-acre campus provides an expansive, yet inclusive environment that lays the foundation for encouraging children to challenge themselves academically, creatively, and physically throughout the summer.

    We are thrilled to present our DIS-Daniel Summer Programs Preview for Summer 2026!

    Click Here for the electronic version of the Summer Programs 2026 brochure.
    Click Here for the Summer Lunch Menu.

    Registration dates and times for DIS-Daniel families will be staggered:
    DIS-Daniel Rising 9th through 12th graders: Tuesday, March 10th, 9:00 a.m.
    DIS-Daniel Rising 5th through 8th graders: Wednesday, March 11th, 9:00 a.m.
    DIS-Daniel Rising K4 through 4th graders: Thursday, March 12th, 9:00 a.m.
    DIS-Daniel Rising K3: Friday, March 13th, 9:00 a.m.

    *The above registration dates and times apply to families who have completed contracts for the 2026-2027 school year.

    Registration will open for families outside of DIS-Daniel on Thursday, April 23rd, 9:00 a.m.

  • Registration Tips and Pointers:

    – Before registration opens, please send any questions you have to summerandauxiliary@dis-gidoti.com . Some camps fill up quickly, so asking questions before registration opens is beneficial.
    – If an offering is full, email summerandauxiliary@dis-gidoti.com your child's name and the name of the camp waitlist. We add names to the waitlists in the order they are received via email.
    – You will receive a confirmation email after registering. If you do not receive one, that means your registration is not complete.
    – New families: Since you have never created a CampBrain account, we highly recommend creating an account prior to registration, so you can see how the registration portal works. To create an account, visit https://dis-daniel-afterschool.campbrainregistration.com/. DIS-Daniel uses CampBrain for summer programs, extended care, after-school programs, Bulldog Athletic Association, Baby Bulldogs, Lads and Dads, and more, so we recommend bookmarking this website.
    – Read over our  CampBrain Overview for Beginners  if you have questions on what each step of registration looks like.

EXTENDED CARE

  • Extended Care is an after-school program that provides daily care for children grades K3-6th as soon as the school day ends and as late as 5:30.

  • How do we sign up?

    All children grades K3-6th are required to sign up for Extended Care and sign the Extended Care Contract. Complete the Extended Care Contract by clicking the sign up online button below.

Sign Up Online

EARLY CARE

  • Early Care is a before-school program that provides daily care for children grades K3-6th from 7:00-7:20 a.m. in the Lower School Library.

    If you are interested in signing up, please email Sarah Forwood at sforwood@dis-gidoti.com. Early Care billing occurs monthly through your FACTS Account, similar to Extended Care.

BULLDOG ATHLETIC ASSOCIATION

  • BAA was established in 2008 with the purpose of promoting and supporting competitive youth sports for students at DIS-Daniel in grades 3 - 6.

    BAA currently fields teams in the following sports: football, cheerleading, cross country, volleyball, basketball (girls & boys), and soccer (girls & boys).

    For more information, please contact Gerald Jones at gjones@dis-gidoti.com.

CREATIVE DANCE CENTER

  • Creative Dance Center offers dance classes after school from K4-12 grade. K3 is offered once a week during school time. They are held on the DIS-Daniel campus. The classes include Tap, Ballet, Pointe, Jazz and Lyrical. The term runs from September through May. A recital is held in May at the Saenger Theatre. For more information, please contact Shay Owen at sowen@dis-gidoti.com.

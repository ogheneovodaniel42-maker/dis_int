Your Child's Educational Journey Awaits.

Daniel International Schoolaccepts students in grades K3-12 from varied backgrounds who will benefit from a challenging academic program and make positive contributions to our school community. Students are admitted on the basis of entrance exams, school records, and interviews or references. Extracurricular interests and talents are also taken into consideration.

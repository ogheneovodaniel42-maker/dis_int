FACTS

All tuition payments to DIS-Daniel will be made through FACTS Tuition Management. Parents need to sign on to FACTS and create and account using the link.

SEE FACTS INFO SHEET

Payment options:

  • Annually

    One payment; Billed on July 1 | Payments are due on the 20th of the Month

  • Semi-Annually

    Two payments; Billed on July 1 and November 1 | Payments are due on the 20th of the Month

  • 10-Month Plan

    1st of each month beginning in July ending in April. Payments are due by the 20th of each month. There is a $25 per month bookkeeping fee associated with the 10-month plan.

Monthly payment plans are required to purchase Tuition Refund Insurance (K3-2nd) - $110 | (3rd-6th) - $125 | (9th-12th) - $135
